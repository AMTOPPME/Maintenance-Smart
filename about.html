Maintenance ONE
To make the team's life easier.

Maintenance ONE is a one-stop digital documentation platform and application for maintenance teams. Its importance lies in three key aspects:

1

Core Concept: To make our team's life easier

The platform reflects its role as a support workstation. It integrates complex procedures, eliminating the tedious work of technicians and engineers searching through different folders for technical documents.
2

Functional Integration: The Pillars of Maintenance

  • 📘 Schematics: Quickly locate PLC systems (F12, F13, F16, F201).
  • 📑 I/O Descriptions: Detailed PLC input/output definition files.
  • 🔧 Procedures: Standardized calibration and PLC SOPs.
  • 🎓 Training: Instant access to materials and instructional videos.
3

Team Collaboration and Professional Image

More than just a platform, it is a professional document library designed to help electrical teams to read electrical documents anytime anywhere via mobile devices. Team involvement and collaboration make this application a reliable companion like an old friend.
